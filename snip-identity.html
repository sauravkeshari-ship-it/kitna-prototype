A subscription is a loop. It repeats until somebody stops it. The mark is that loop with a piece cut free.

The mark

SVG, single path pair

Most cut-something apps reach for scissors, which say cutting but say nothing about what is being cut. This mark carries both halves of the idea at once: a closed ring is a charge that comes back every month, and the displaced arc is the moment it stops coming back. The small piece sits slightly rotated and lifted, so it reads as freshly cut rather than as a gap that was always there.

It is two arcs of one circle, radius 15 on a 48 grid, stroke 6.4, round caps. Nothing else. No scissors, no rupee sign, no wordplay that needs explaining.

It has to hold at 16px

Notification icon size

The cut piece survives to 16px because it is displaced, not merely separated. A gap alone would close up at small sizes and the mark would read as a plain ring. Above 96px, switch to the thin weight so the stroke does not look bloated.

Thin weight, stroke 4. Use above 96px only: billboards, splash screens, print.

Clear space

One stroke width, all round

Keep 6.4 units clear on every side, measured from the 48 grid, not from the ink. The cut piece already sits near the top edge, so crowding it there is what breaks the mark first.

What breaks it

Four to avoid
Correct
One colour, cut piece sprung free.
Never
Two colours. The cut piece is not a highlight, it is part of the same object.
Never
Piece put back. Now it is a closed ring, which is the opposite of the idea.
Never
Thin weight at small sizes. It disappears against anything but white.
Never
Rotated. The cut belongs at the top, where a thread would be held to be cut.
Never
Filled as a solid donut. The stroke and the caps are the whole drawing.

Inherited from the product

The identity does not introduce a palette. It uses the one the app already runs on, so the icon, the launch screen and the interface are the same system.

Splash

Full Rausch, white lockup. The cut piece springs free over 0.95s. The screen holds for 2s in total, then hands off to onboarding.

The splash is the only place the mark moves. It starts as a closed ring and the piece detaches, which states the whole product in under a second and never needs repeating. Everywhere else the mark is already cut and perfectly still.
